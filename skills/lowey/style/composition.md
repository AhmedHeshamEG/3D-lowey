# Composition

## Thirds and centre

- Put the subject on a third (the report says `left third`, `centre`, `right third`, or `off the grid`).
- Centre deliberately: symmetry, confrontation, a reveal, a title moment.
- Off the grid is a mistake unless the frame is about emptiness.

## Headroom and lead room

- A character's head 5–15% from the top in medium and full shots; less (or cut at the forehead) in close-ups.
- Lead room: leave space in front of where a character looks or moves (subject on the left third looking right).

## Tangents

Edges that just touch — the frame's border, each other, the horizon — create tension that reads as a mistake. The
report lists border tangents; clear them by 5% or overlap decisively.

## Depth layering

Foreground, middle, background:
- **Fore**: something soft and dark at the frame's edge (a chair back, a plant) gives depth.
- **Middle**: the subject.
- **Back**: the set, lower contrast (fog, cooler, darker).
The `top` diagram shows the layers; if everything sits on one line, move something forward or back.

## Clutter

≤ 7 significant elements. Empty space has a job: it frames the subject, gives lead room, or holds an overlay.

## Vertical (9:16)

The subject in the middle 60% vertically, away from the bottom 22% (captions and buttons) and the right edge (likes and
comments). `observe(framing="9:16")` before you call a vertical shot done.
