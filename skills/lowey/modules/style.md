# Style (how 3D-lowey videos look)

- **Characters:** blobs (see `characters.md`). One visual family for everyone.
- **World:** low-poly, big simple shapes, the project's palette (`palette:N`). Few props, strong silhouettes.
- **Light:** one warm key on the subject, a cooler world around it. Dusk/night moods read best with one warm source.
  Glow (`"glow": 1–4`) on light sources and magic things: it now casts a halo.
- **Overlays:** statements, not decoration: the big X, a question mark, one title, a label that follows a thing.
  White text, bold, one or two words.
- **Finish:** `look` post "cinematic" for story beats; ink outlines (`outline`) suit the cartoon characters.
- **Framing:** subject in the centre third (both 16:9 and 9:16 export), 35–50 mm for characters, close-ups at 85 mm
  for reactions.
- **Pacing:** 2–6 s per shot, cut on new ideas, one camera move per shot, holds on punchlines.
