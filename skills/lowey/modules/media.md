# Media in the shot — photos, video, Manim

## Pictures and video (`add_media`)

- **Card** (default): a thin card standing in the 3D world — a photo on a desk, a poster on a wall, a screen's
  content. Place it like any object afterwards (`place … on`, `facing` the camera).
- **Overlay** (`overlay=True`): flat over the frame — screenshots, transparent graphics, a logo.
- Videos play from `at` seconds on the timeline (default: the playhead). Start them on the word that introduces them.

## Manim (`render_manim`)

Graphs, equations and diagrams rendered on the laptop and laid over the shot, transparent by default so they float over
the 3D world. Pass the source and the Scene class; keep Manim's palette close to the project's (`read_project` lists it).

- Start the overlay on the word that introduces the idea; keep it until the idea is done; let it leave before the cut.
- Keep the 3D shot simple behind it: a held medium shot, the subject off to one side so the graphic has room.
- One graphic at a time.

## Don't

- Don't paste a screenshot when the Kit can build the thing (a desk, a computer).
- Don't cover the subject's face with an overlay.
